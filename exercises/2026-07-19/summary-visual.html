Gaps · prioritized

Critical

Crown-jewel blind spot

The three techniques Unit 42 says defenders must catch — AppDomainManager hijacking, DLL side-loading, ETW tamper — produced zero telemetry. Sysmon EID 7 (image/module load) was absent entirely.

High

SIEM correlation unconfirmed

No live SIEM connection this run — correlation queries are staged but not executed. Half of the validation remains theoretical.

Medium

Proxy ≠ real TTP

The one detected technique used a PowerShell cradle; the actual campaign delivers via certutil / browser chain. Representative, not exact.

Recommendations

01

Close the visibility gap. Collect & forward Sysmon (EID 7, 13) and Security (4688, 4698) logs — then re-run to prove crown-jewel coverage.

02

Confirm SIEM coverage. Run the staged queries; verify PowerShell script-block logging is enabled and flowing fleet-wide.

03

Tune EDR. Treat DLL sideloading and AppDomainManager hijacking as high-risk — signed binaries loading untrusted modules.

04

Verify & re-run. Confirm the disputed Atomic test IDs against a real checkout, then repeat the exercise for full coverage.
